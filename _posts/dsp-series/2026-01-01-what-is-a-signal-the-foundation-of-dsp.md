## The Ubiquitous Nature of Signals

Signals are everywhere. From the electrical impulses firing in your neurons as you read this, to the Wi-Fi packets carrying this article through the air, to seismic waves traveling through Earth's crust—every physical phenomenon that varies with time, space, or any independent variable is a **signal**.

In engineering terms, a signal is **any physical quantity that varies with respect to one or more independent variables** (time, space, distance, temperature) and **carries information**. Mathematically, it's expressed as a function of one or more independent variables.

**Examples:**
- $s_1(t) = 5t$ — a signal varying with time $t$
- $s_2(x) = 2x^2$ — a signal varying with spatial coordinate $x$
- $f(x, y) = 2x^2 + 3y$ — a multivariable signal depending on two spatial coordinates
